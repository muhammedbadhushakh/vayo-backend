How We Began

A Simple Idea

Vayo Collections began with a simple idea — beautiful ornaments shouldn't be reserved for special occasions alone. We curate every piece for the woman who wants to feel adorned on an ordinary Tuesday and radiant at a wedding, without compromise on quality or price.

From our design table to your doorstep, every piece is quality-checked, boxed with care, and shipped across India. We work with skilled artisans to bring you kundan, polki, meenakari and antique-finish pieces at prices that don't ask you to choose between beauty and budget.
