Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you browse Vayo Collections or place an order, we collect information such as your name, email address, phone number, shipping address and order details. We also automatically collect basic device and browsing information to keep the site secure and working smoothly.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process and deliver your orders
  • To communicate order updates, shipping and delivery status
  • To respond to customer service requests
  • To send occasional offers or newsletters, only if you've opted in
  • To improve our products, website and overall shopping experience

3. Payment Information

All payments are processed securely by Razorpay. We never see or store your full card, UPI or bank details on our servers — Razorpay handles this in compliance with PCI-DSS standards.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your account and order data is stored securely using Firebase, with access restricted through authentication and security rules so that only you and authorised administrators can view your information.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We only share necessary details with trusted service providers — such as payment processors and delivery partners — solely to fulfil your order.

6. Your Choices

You may update your account details at any time, unsubscribe from marketing emails, or contact us to request deletion of your account and associated data, subject to legal record-keeping requirements.

7. Contact Us

For any privacy-related questions, please reach out via our Contact page.
